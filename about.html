Our story

The Palm Beach team grew up in Fort Myers going to local high schools like Riverdale and Fort Myers High School. Their families live in Buckingham, Alva, and Fort Myers. They’ve watched Lee County grow for over 30 years. More people, more neighborhoods, more boats and RVs, and not enough covered places to keep them.

They weren’t outsiders who spotted a market. They were locals who watched their own community change and decided to build something it actually needed. So they did themselves with a local construction company.

Prior to Palm Beach RV and Boat Storage, the family operated residential and commercial rental properties in the area. This is not their first business. It is their most personal one.

“Our mission is simple: To provide the safest, most convenient home away from home for your next adventure.”
