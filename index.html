Outdoor storage

RVs. Boats. Everything in between.

Covered storage on Palm Beach Boulevard in Fort Myers, designed from the ground up for large vehicles, not retrofitted from a parking lot.

RV Storage

Most neighborhoods in Fort Myers weren’t laid out with a big rig in mind. If the driveway isn’t going to work, this is where your RV goes. Every spot is covered. I-75 is close in both directions. And if backing in isn’t something you want to deal with, we have pull-through spots for that.

Boat Storage

We’re up the street from the Caloosahatchee River. Hankley’s Marina, Davis Boat Ramp, Oasis Boat Ramp, all close by. Store your boat here and the water’s never far. We’ve got an enclosed wash bay with a motor flush station on site, so you can rinse the hull and flush the engine before you leave. One stop.

Other Vehicles We Store

Cars, trucks, trailers, and ATVs. If it can be driven on a Florida road, it fits here. Our clearance height is 15 feet, which covers anything street-legal. The one thing we don’t take is food trucks or food trailers.

Amenities

Built for boats and RVs. Not a converted parking lot.

Ten canopy structures. A fully paved lot. Concrete block perimeter walls with a single gated entry point. Wide drive lanes sized for Class A motorhomes, fifth wheels, and trailered boats up to the legal road limit.

Electric Included

Electric is included in the monthly rate, at no extra fee. Buildings 2, 3, and 5 have 20-amp outlets. All other buildings have 30-amp. Keeps your batteries conditioned and your systems running between trips.

Boat Wash Bay

Built for washing down your boat and flushing the motor after a saltwater trip on the river or the Gulf. Enclosed wash bay with a motor flush station on site.

Ice Machine

200-lb Ice Machine: Grab a bag on your way to the ramp.

Waste Dump Station

Take care of the tank before you head home. The wash bay, dump station, and service area are at the rear of the property.

About us

Born here. Built here.

The team brings together alumni from both Fort Myers High School and Riverdale High School. With deep family ties reaching from Buckingham and Alva to the heart of Fort Myers, their local heritage is a cornerstone of the group. They’ve watched Lee County grow for over 30 years. More people, more neighborhoods, more boats and RVs, and not enough covered places to keep them.

So they built one. Did it themselves. Local crew, local labor.

Family-owned, family-run, with someone on site every day of the week. Not a chain. Not an out-of-town investment. The people who built it are the same ones running it.
