The property

Outdoor Storage Facility

Amenities built from the ground up for boats and RVs. Not a converted parking lot.

Built for RVs, boats, and other recreational vehicles

Covered outdoor storage on Palm Beach Boulevard

Palm Beach RV and Boat Storage is a covered outdoor storage facility on Palm Beach Boulevard in Fort Myers, Florida. Ten canopy structures. A fully paved lot. Concrete block perimeter walls with a single gated entry point. Wide drive lanes sized for Class A motorhomes, fifth wheels, and trailered boats up to the legal road limit.

The facility is family-owned and operated by Fort Myers natives with over 30 years in Lee County. It was designed and built locally, not contracted out. Staff is on site every day of the week. The wash bay, dump station, and service area are at the rear of the property. The office is at the front.

Facility amenities

What comes with your spot

Covered Storage

Every spot has a roof over it. Sun and rain protection comes standard, not as an upgrade.

Paved

Clean, solid surface throughout the facility.

Gated & Fenced

A full concrete block perimeter with one way in and one way out. That's the front gate.

Keypad Access

Every tenant gets their own individual code.

24 hour security

Full surveillance runs throughout the facility. Well lit at night.

Electric Included

Electric is included in the monthly rate, at no extra fee. Buildings 2, 3, and 5 have 20-amp outlets. All other buildings have 30-amp. Keeps your batteries conditioned and your systems running between trips.

Boat Wash Bay

Built for washing down your boat and flushing the motor after a saltwater trip on the river or the Gulf. Enclosed wash bay with a motor flush station on site.

Ice Machine

200-lb Ice Machine: Grab a bag on your way to the ramp.

Waste Dump Station

Take care of the tank before you head home. The wash bay, dump station, and service area are at the rear of the property.

Air Compressor

Top off the tires before you pull out.

Someone On Site Daily

Staff is here Monday through Sunday during business hours. A real person to talk to if you need one.

15-foot clearance

Our clearance height is 15 feet, which covers anything street-legal.
